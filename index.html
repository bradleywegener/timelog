How to use timelog

Record what you are doing, review where your time went, and fill in gaps later. Everything stays in this browser.

track time
  1. Choose a category and optionally enter an activity name.
  2. Press Enter in the activity field, or choose Start Timer.
  3. While recording, use Stop & Save or press Ctrl+Enter on Windows/Linux, or Cmd+Enter on macOS.
  4. The active card shows elapsed time, start time, current category, and activity name.
categories
  • Categories identify the kind of time you are recording.
  • While recording, select the current category to change it without stopping the timer.
  • Add, edit, recolour, or delete categories from the hamburger menu.
  • Deleting a category moves its existing activities to another category you choose.
untracked time
  • Time between activities is shown as untracked.
  • Choose an untracked period to classify it with a category and optional description.
  • Long untracked periods can be split into two activities with separate times, names, and categories.
  • Deleting a tracked activity turns its time back into untracked time.
review your time
  • Track shows the current timer and latest activities.
  • Log shows the full activity history, including the current activity.
  • Insights shows daily category totals, weekly breakdowns, and all-time statistics.
  • Activities crossing midnight are split between the affected days.
keyboard shortcuts
Tab
Move through available controls.
Enter
Select a category or start a timer from the activity field.
Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter
Stop and save the active timer.
Escape
Close the active dialog.
settings and offline use
  • Switch between light, dark, and system appearance in the hamburger menu.
  • Choose 12-hour or 24-hour time display.
  • Enable notifications to see the active activity while the app is in the background.
  • timelog works offline after its app files have been cached as a PWA.
  • Your entries are stored in this browser and are not synchronised between devices.
